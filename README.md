Anonymous Reporting Program
(Spring 2022 - ITP 116 Final Project)

Overall  Description:
* College campuses are known to be centers for debate, knowledge development, and character growth. Unfortunately, many harmful incidents infiltrate these spaces. The difficulties arise from fear of reporting such incidents, lack of knowledge of appropriate reporting and resource services, and unreported data.
* This program will track and document these types of incidents on the University of Southern California campus and provide ready-access information on resources for victims of harmful incidents on and around the university campus
